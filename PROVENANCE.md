# Provenance and attribution

This is a selected, adapted portfolio edition of the team solution **«Радар влияния» (Business Impact Radar)** for the Alfa Future hackathon, Alfa-Bank case. It is not an official Alfa-Bank product and does not imply endorsement or employment by the bank.

The original team authored the retained profiling, event-contract and baseline-matching modules. The portfolio edition adds synthetic inputs, a standalone demonstration, English documentation and packaging; it adjusts package imports, removes a fixed cohort rendering assertion and a historical working-document gap, and excludes the separate experimental matching branch. Team attribution is retained; sole authorship of the full application is not claimed.

Organizer datasets and their derived company profiles are excluded. No bank research excerpts, publisher article archives, historical traces, saved model responses, gateway credentials or team working documents are distributed. Example URLs use example.com, and demo companies and news are invented.

Third-party libraries are installed from their normal package distributions: NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), pandas (BSD-3-Clause), Pydantic (MIT) and pytest (MIT). This repository does not redistribute their source or assign a new license to the team's own code.
